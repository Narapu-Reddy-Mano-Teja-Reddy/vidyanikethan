Detailed Overview

Master Science, Engineering & Logic at Vidyaniketan

The MPC (Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry) stream at Vidyaniketan Jr College is structured to provide an intensive, concept-driven learning framework. Mathematics develops logical problem-solving, Physics explains physical phenomena and mechanics, while Chemistry provides deep insights into molecular interactions and reactions.

Our teaching methodology balances Board exam syllabus with entrance test speed and accuracy training. Daily practice sheets, topic-wise assessments, and dedicated doubt-clearing sessions ensure every student achieves maximum performance.

Curriculum & Subjects

MPC Detailed Course Structure

Complete subject distribution for 1st Year (Junior Intermediate) and 2nd Year (Senior Intermediate).

1st Year (Junior Inter)

Year 1
  • Mathematics 1A: Functions, Mathematical Induction, Matrices, Addition of Vectors, Trigonometry & Hyperbolic Functions.
  • Mathematics 1B: 2D Coordinate Geometry, Straight Lines, Pair of Straight Lines, 3D Geometry, Limits & Differentiation.
  • Physics (Paper 1): Physical World, Units & Measurements, Kinematics, Laws of Motion, Work Energy & Power, Gravitation, Thermodynamics & Sound.
  • Chemistry (Paper 1): Atomic Structure, Periodic Table, Chemical Bonding, States of Matter, Stoichiometry, Thermodynamics & Organic Chemistry Basics.
  • Languages: General English & Second Language (Telugu / Hindi / Sanskrit).

2nd Year (Senior Inter)

Year 2
  • Mathematics 2A: Complex Numbers, De Moivre’s Theorem, Quadratic Expressions, Permutations & Combinations, Probability & Binomial Theorem.
  • Mathematics 2B: Circles, System of Circles, Parabola, Ellipse, Hyperbola, Integration & Differential Equations.
  • Physics (Paper 2): Waves, Ray & Wave Optics, Electrostatics, Current Electricity, Magnetism, Electromagnetic Induction & Semiconductor Physics + Practical Labs.
  • Chemistry (Paper 2): Solid State, Solutions, Electrochemistry, Surface Chemistry, Metallurgy, p-Block & d-Block Elements, Organic Reactions + Practical Labs.
  • Languages: Advanced English & Second Language.
