CEC Stream (Civics, Economics & Commerce)

A versatile humanities and business stream designed for future Administrators, Lawyers, Civil Servants (UPSC/APPSC), Accountants, and Business Leaders.

Stream Overview

Master Governance, Economy & Business at Vidyaniketan

The CEC (Civics, Economics, Commerce) stream at Vidyaniketan Jr College is structured to provide students with a comprehensive understanding of democratic governance, constitutional frameworks, national economic policies, and commercial business practices.

Civics instils deep knowledge of Indian Government, Political Science, and Rights. Economics covers market trends and public finance, while Commerce trains students in business management and financial bookkeeping. We conduct regular debates, general knowledge workshops, and foundational Civil Services/CLAT guidance.

Civics (Political Science & Governance) Economics (Micro & Macro Economics) Commerce & Bookkeeping English & Languages

Curriculum & Subjects

CEC Detailed Course Structure

Complete subject distribution for 1st Year (Junior Intermediate) and 2nd Year (Senior Intermediate).

1st Year (Junior Inter)

Year 1
  • Civics / Political Science (Paper 1): Concepts of Political Science, State, Sovereignty, Law & Liberty, Citizenship, Fundamental Rights, Constitution & Governance.
  • Economics (Paper 1): Micro Economic Theory, Utility Analysis, Demand & Supply, Cost & Revenue Concepts, Price Determination & National Income.
  • Commerce & Accountancy (Paper 1): Fundamentals of Commerce, Forms of Business Organisations, Sole Trading, Partnerships, Journal, Ledger & Trial Balance.
  • Languages: General English & Second Language (Telugu / Hindi / Sanskrit).

2nd Year (Senior Inter)

Year 2
  • Civics / Political Science (Paper 2): Indian Constitution, Fundamental Duties, Union & State Executive, Parliament, Judiciary, Local Self Government & AP State Administration.
  • Economics (Paper 2): Macro Economics, Indian Economic Development, Agriculture & Industrial Growth, Population, Poverty & Public Finance.
  • Commerce & Accountancy (Paper 2): Financial Markets, Stock Exchange, Banking Services, Partnership Accounts, Consignment & Company Accounts.
  • Languages: Advanced English & Second Language.
