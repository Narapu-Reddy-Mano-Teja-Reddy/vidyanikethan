BiPC Stream (Biology, Physics & Chemistry)

The primary foundation for future Doctors, Healthcare Professionals, Pharmacists, and Life Science Researchers. Includes integrated NEET-UG preparation and practical laboratory experiments.

Stream Overview

Prepare for Medical Excellence at Vidyaniketan

The BiPC (Biology, Physics, Chemistry) stream at Vidyaniketan Jr College is tailored for students passionate about life, human anatomy, plant and animal biology, and health sciences. Botany and Zoology build a solid understanding of biological systems, while Physics and Chemistry provide essential analytical grounding required for medical entrance exams.

At Vidyaniketan, our BiPC curriculum features state-of-the-art Science Laboratories with compound microscopes, preserved biological specimens, dissection kits, chemical apparatus, and safety equipment. Our NEET faculty conducts weekly NCERT-focused mock tests and speed practice sessions.

Curriculum & Subjects

BiPC Detailed Course Structure

Complete subject distribution for 1st Year (Junior Intermediate) and 2nd Year (Senior Intermediate).

1st Year (Junior Inter)

Year 1
  • Botany (Paper 1): Diversity in Living World, Plant Kingdom, Morphology of Flowering Plants, Cell Structure & Division, Plant Taxonomy & Embryology.
  • Zoology (Paper 1): Diversity of Animal World, Structural Organisation in Animals, Biomolecules, Animal Diversity - Invertebrates & Chordates, Ecology & Environment.
  • Physics (Paper 1): Kinematics, Laws of Motion, Work, Energy & Power, Gravitation, Mechanical Properties of Solids & Fluids, Thermal Properties of Matter & Thermodynamics.
  • Chemistry (Paper 1): Atomic Structure, Classification of Elements, Chemical Bonding, States of Matter, Chemical Equilibrium, Redox Reactions & Organic Chemistry Basics.
  • Languages: General English & Second Language (Telugu / Hindi / Sanskrit).

2nd Year (Senior Inter)

Year 2
  • Botany (Paper 2): Plant Physiology (Photosynthesis, Respiration, Growth Hormones), Microbiology, Genetics, Molecular Biology & Biotechnology Principles + Practicals.
  • Zoology (Paper 2): Human Anatomy & Physiology (Digestion, Breathing, Circulation, Excretion, Nervous & Reproductive Systems), Genetics, Organic Evolution & Applied Zoology + Practicals.
  • Physics (Paper 2): Waves, Ray Optics, Electric Charges & Fields, Current Electricity, Magnetism, Electromagnetic Waves & Semiconductor Electronics + Practicals.
  • Chemistry (Paper 2): Solutions, Electrochemistry, Surface Chemistry, Metallurgy, p-Block & d-Block Elements, Coordination Compounds, Biomolecules & Organic Chemistry + Practicals.
  • Languages: Advanced English & Second Language.
