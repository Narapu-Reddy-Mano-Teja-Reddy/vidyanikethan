COMMERCE & ANALYTICS

MEC Stream (Mathematics, Economics & Commerce)

The perfect combination of mathematical precision, economic principles, and business acumen. Ideal for aspiring Chartered Accountants (CA), Financial Analysts, and Business Executives.

Stream Overview

Master Finance, Commerce & Analytics at Vidyaniketan

The MEC (Mathematics, Economics, Commerce) stream at Vidyaniketan Jr College blends quantitative mathematics with modern business and economic theory. This unique combination gives students a competitive edge in professional accounting courses (CA, CMA, CS) as well as data analytics, banking, and corporate finance.

Our faculty focuses on real-world accounting principles, double-entry bookkeeping, financial statement analysis, micro & macro economics, and mathematical problem-solving. We provide integrated CA Foundation and CLAT orientation to prepare students for top professional career entrance exams right alongside their Board studies.

Mathematics 1A, 1B / 2A, 2B Economics (Micro & Macro) Commerce & Accountancy English & Languages

Curriculum & Subjects

MEC Detailed Course Structure

Complete subject distribution for 1st Year (Junior Intermediate) and 2nd Year (Senior Intermediate).

1st Year (Junior Inter)

Year 1
  • Mathematics 1A & 1B: Functions, Matrices, Trigonometry, 2D Straight Lines, Limits, Differentiation & 3D Geometry.
  • Economics (Paper 1): Micro Economics, Consumer Behaviour, Demand & Supply Analysis, Production Theory, Market Structures & National Income.
  • Commerce & Accountancy (Paper 1): Business Concepts, Sole Proprietorship, Joint Stock Companies, Bookkeeping, Journal, Ledger, Trial Balance & Bank Reconciliation.
  • Languages: General English & Second Language (Telugu / Hindi / Sanskrit).

2nd Year (Senior Inter)

Year 2
  • Mathematics 2A & 2B: Permutations & Combinations, Probability, Statistics, Circles, Parabola, Integration & Differential Equations.
  • Economics (Paper 2): Macro Economics, Indian Economy, Agriculture, Industry, Infrastructure, Banking, Public Finance & AP Economic Development.
  • Commerce & Accountancy (Paper 2): Financial Markets, Banking Services, Partnership Accounts, Consignment Accounts, Non-Profit Organisations & Company Accounts.
  • Languages: Advanced English & Second Language.

Career Pathways for MEC Graduates

The MEC stream opens doors to prestigious accounting credentials, financial engineering, management, and legal professions.

Chartered Accountancy (CA)

Chartered Accountant credential through ICAI (Foundation, Intermediate & Final). High prestige and financial reward.

Premier Designation

Cost & Management Accounting (CMA / CS)

Corporate Governance
