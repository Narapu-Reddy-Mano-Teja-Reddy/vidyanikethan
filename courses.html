Intermediate Academic Streams

Explore our carefully designed Intermediate streams tailored to prepare you for competitive entrance exams and higher professional degrees.

MPC (Mathematics, Physics & Chemistry)

The MPC stream is a rigorous program designed for students with strong analytical skills. Serves as a gateway to top engineering programs, Computer Science, AI, and competitive entrance exams like JEE Main, Advanced, and AP EAMCET.

Subjects:
  • • Mathematics (1A, 1B / 2A, 2B)
  • • Physics (Theory & Practicals)
  • • Chemistry (Theory & Practicals)
Career Opportunities:
Engineering Artificial Intelligence Data Science Architecture Defence Services
Explore Full MPC Course Details

BiPC (Biology, Physics & Chemistry)

The BiPC stream is the path for students aspiring for medicine, healthcare, life sciences, and biological research. Prepares students for medical entrance exams like NEET-UG, Pharmacy, Biotechnology, and Agricultural Sciences.

Subjects:
  • • Botany (Plant Biology)
  • • Zoology (Animal Biology & Anatomy)
  • • Physics & Chemistry (Theory & Practicals)
Career Opportunities:
MBBS / BDS Pharmacy (Pharm.D) Biotechnology Agriculture Veterinary
Explore Full BiPC Course Details

MEC (Mathematics, Economics & Commerce)

The MEC stream offers a strategic blend of analytical mathematics and commerce studies. Perfect for professional paths like Chartered Accountancy (CA), Cost Accounting (CMA), banking, financial analytics, and business management.

Subjects:
  • • Mathematics (1A, 1B / 2A, 2B)
  • • Economics (Micro & Macro)
  • • Commerce & Accountancy
Career Opportunities:
Chartered Accountancy (CA) Cost Accounting (CMA) Banking & Finance Data Analytics
Explore Full MEC Course Details

CEC (Civics, Economics & Commerce)

The CEC stream provides a comprehensive education in business practices, economic systems, and civic administration. Prepares students for Civil Services (UPSC/APPSC), Law (CLAT), Public Administration, B.Com, and BBA.

Subjects:
  • • Civics (Political Science & Governance)
  • • Economics (Micro & Macro)
  • • Commerce & Accountancy
Career Opportunities:
Law (BA LLB) Civil Services Public Administration Business Management
Explore Full CEC Course Details
